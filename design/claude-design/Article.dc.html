More than 100 teenage gang members stage armed display in Kadamtali

Residents say such displays happen regularly and that police did not respond despite being informed.

DC Dhaka Chronicles13 Nov 2026 · 2 min read
পড়ুন বাংলায়

More than 100 teenage gang members staged an armed display in Dhaka’s Kadamtali area on Friday afternoon, causing fear among local residents.

Witnesses said the youths carried machetes, hockey sticks and knives while marching through Palashpur Road No. 4, near Smritidhara PNP School and other areas at around 4:15 pm.

Residents alleged that such displays happen regularly and claimed police failed to respond despite being informed. Some parents also said gang leaders used phone calls to recruit teenagers.

Police have been sent to the area and will collect CCTV footage, the officer-in-charge said.

Kadamtali Police Station Officer-in-Charge Sheikh Ashrafuzzaman said police had been sent to the area. He added that officers would collect CCTV footage and take action against those involved.
