# Dhaka Chronicles — newsroom guide

Sign in at `/admin` (staff only). Every action shows a message when it succeeds or fails, and important events are posted to the team's Discord channels.

## Stories
- **Articles → New article** to write a story. Save as draft, send for review, schedule or publish.
- **Instagram** (main admins only): pick posts from @dhakachronicles, then import as drafts or import and publish. Sections and tags are added automatically.
- **Translate:** on any article, use **Translate to Bangla automatically** (or English). This creates a draft in the other language; check it before publishing. **Write … by hand** creates an empty draft instead.
- **Featured / homepage highlight:** pin stories from the Overview page or the article list. The site also features the week's most-liked Instagram posts on its own.
- **Reader error reports:** every story has a "Report an error" link. Reports arrive in the Discord alerts channel with a link to the story's editor.

## Money
- **Brand deals** is the record of every paid partnership (stages, fees, invoices; staff only). To use a deal as proof of your work, open it and tick **Show on the website (portfolio)**, then add the logo, a one-line description and a link to the post. It then appears under "Brands we've worked with" on the homepage and the Advertise page. Fees, contacts and notes are never public.
- **Ads** are website banners. Create an ad, add the link and dates, and upload any of the three sizes:
  - Desktop banner: 1200 × 300
  - Phone banner: 640 × 320
  - Square: 600 × 600 (inside articles)

  The site shows the right size per screen and labels it "Advertisement". Each ad counts views and clicks, and has a private **report link** you can send to the advertiser.
- **Enquiries:** the form on the Advertise page creates a new lead in Brand deals and posts to Discord.

## People
- **Team page:** who appears on the public "Our team" page (photo, name and role in both languages, bio, Instagram, order, show/hide).
- **Staff:** sign-in accounts and roles. Main admins can reset passwords; nobody can change the main admins.
- **Authors:** the bylines shown on stories.
